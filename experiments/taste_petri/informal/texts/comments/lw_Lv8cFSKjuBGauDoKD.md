HIS COMMENT:
I'd like to see discussion of data rather than mostly a priori argument ("I have a sense" ... "I suspect desire"). For aggregate data, there's SSC survey and there are studies of "ambitious" groups (e.g. the [Harvard Men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grant_Study) study, Benbow on precious math talent). There are also anecdata of the exceptionally ambitious. E.g. Musk had first child age ~30 and has many kids, [Hassabis](https://www.standard.co.uk/lifestyle/london-life/exclusive-interview-meet-demis-hassabis-london-s-megamind-who-just-sold-his-company-to-google-for-ps400m-9098707.html) had first child aged ~29. It seems Jaan Tallinn had kids starting in his 20s before founding Skype ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaan_Tallinn)). Bezos has 4 kids (started age 37). Gates has 3 kids (started age ~40). Turing award-winners David Patterson and Judea Pearl had kids in their 20s before their biggest contributions. Yoshua Bengio in his 30s. etc
