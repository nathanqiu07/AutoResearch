PARENT (not his words):
Does (or could) your analysis distinguish between "someone in the household" with, say, "someone you are sleeping in the same bed as"? It's very common for relationships within a household to be highly varied in ways that seem relevant.

HIS COMMENT:
There is a small number of studies that distinguish spouse from other relationships. See Figure S5 of this [paper](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2020.05.21.20108746v1). I don't think there's enough data to draw a strong empirical conclusion. Most of our data for estimating SAR is from China/Korea/Taiwan and I'd guess these are mostly nuclear families or extended family (not many group house / flatmates).
