On October 12, 2026, Larkspur Workspaces Companies LLC (“Larkspur Obligor”) entered into a satisfaction letter (the “Satisfaction Letter”) with Meridian Horizon Fund III-4 L.P. (“MHF”), Halvorsen Pierce International Bank, as senior tranche administrative agent, Tidewater Agency Services Limited, as junior tranche administrative agent, and certain other issuing creditors and L/C participants party thereto, each of which is a party to that certain Credit Agreement, dated as of March 14, 2021 (as amended or otherwise modified from time to time, the “Credit Agreement”). Pursuant to the Satisfaction Letter, MHF will pay certain amounts and deposit cash collateral in order to effect a Date of Full Satisfaction (as defined in the Credit Agreement) and become subrogated to the rights of the secured parties under the Credit Agreement. Halvorsen Pierce International Bank, Tidewater Agency Services Limited and certain issuing creditors and L/C participants party to the Satisfaction Letter, constituting the requisite Required L/C Participants (as defined in the Credit Agreement), agreed to forbear the exercise of any rights or remedies against the Larkspur Obligor or the Larkspur Collateral (as defined in the Credit Agreement) with respect to the specified defaults set forth therein, from the date of the Satisfaction Letter while MHF’s payment of amounts and cash collateralization contemplated thereunder is pending, except that such forbearance shall terminate if MHF does not make such payment and cash collateralization by October 18, 2026 or earlier if the Forbearance Agreement is terminated or certain restructuring events occur.

Regulation FD Disclosure.

The Company has elected to withhold interest payment of approximately $4.7 million payable in cash (the “Unsecured Notes Interest Payment”) on October 15, 2026 with respect to the Issuers’ 6.625% Senior Notes due 2028 (the “Unsecured Notes”). While the Company has the liquidity to make the Unsecured Notes Interest Payment, under the indenture governing the Unsecured Notes, the Company has a 30-day grace period to make the Unsecured Notes Interest Payment before such non-payment constitutes an “event of default” with respect to the Unsecured Notes.
