2.4.4 Insider Trading
['34 Act, Section 10 and Rule 10b-5]


Associates are prohibited from effecting transactions based on knowledge of material, non-public
information. The chapter INSIDER TRADING explains K7VM’s policy and all associates are expected to be
familiar with the policy.
