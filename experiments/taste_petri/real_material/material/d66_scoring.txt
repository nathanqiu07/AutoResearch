How We Score and Grade
The Metropolitan Office of Food Safety Compliance inspects about 24,000 restaurants a year to monitor compliance with City and State food safety
regulations. Since July 2010, the Metropolitan Office of Food Safety Compliance has required restaurants to post letter grades showing sanitary inspection
results. Restaurants with a score between 0 and 13 points earn an A, those with 14 to 27 points receive a B and those with 28
or more a C. Inspection results are posted on the Metropolitan Office of Food Safety Compliance's website.
