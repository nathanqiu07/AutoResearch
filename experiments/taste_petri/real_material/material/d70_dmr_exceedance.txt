       Dear Ms. Taylor:

       The Water Quality Division (Division) has reviewed the 2021 and 2022 Discharge Monitoring Reports
       (DMRs) for the permit issued to your company. A concern noted as a result of DMR review included the
       reoccurring exceedance(s) of Sulfide, Barium, Radium, and Temperature. Please refer to the attached
       table to see specific exceedances.

       This letter hereby serves as notification that we have noticed these continued exceedances of the
       described parameters, which constitute violations of the Wyoming Environmental Quality Act (Act), the
       Wyoming Water Quality Rules and Regulations (WWQRR), and respective permit conditions.

       Please submit a thorough update to the Division on the steps being taken to resolve these effluent issues
       and an update of the water treatment plan and/or actions being taken to prevent these exceedances from
       occurring again in the future.

       The written response shall be received by the Division on or before 30 days from the date on this letter.
       The information shall be addressed to Katherine N. McCrea, Water Quality Division, 200 West 17th
       Street, Suite 400, Cheyenne, WY 82002.

       The Water Quality Division is attempting to resolve this violation through conference and conciliation.
       This letter does not constitute an order, but rather, identifies steps to be taken by your company to
       satisfactorily resolve the violation. The intent of this letter is to provide an opportunity for your company
       to show good faith efforts toward resolving the problem and to prevent the need for more formal
       enforcement action by this office.
