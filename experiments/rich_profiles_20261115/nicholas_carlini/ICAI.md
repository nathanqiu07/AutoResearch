## Article II — Define the property before optimizing its measurement

**When a claim uses terms such as robustness, privacy, extraction, or safety, first specify the success event, comparison population, adversary, and acceptable utility.**

This principle appears in distinctions that materially change experimental design. High task accuracy and high behavioral fidelity are different extraction objectives: a useful substitute need not reproduce the victim’s errors. Membership inference, verbatim extraction, approximate reproduction, and counterfactual memorization similarly concern different properties. Their measurements should not be substituted merely because all involve training data. [research__1909_01838_high_accuracy_and_high_fidelity_extract_e395dac6] [research__counterfactual_memorization_in_neural_language_mod_e4f0a48b]

The same discipline limits norm-based robustness. Work on excessive invariance shows that a classifier can preserve its prediction while humans judge that the class has changed. A certificate can remain mathematically valid while its semantic interpretation fails. The unrestricted-adversarial-examples proposal consequently permits abstention but requires useful clean-input coverage, preventing an always-abstain system from satisfying the intended objective. [research__fundamental_tradeoffs_between_invariance_and_sensi_ee0b16ca] [research__unrestricted_adversarial_examples_156d1cae]

Operational specificity does not require pretending every important concept is fully formalizable. The LLM-security position paper distinguishes practical demonstrations of clear harm from research claiming cumulative scientific understanding. The former may remain worthwhile under imperfect definitions; the latter needs scoped problems that support meaningful comparisons. [research__position_adversarial_ml_for_llms_is_not_making_any_580cc8c8]

**Exception and amendment.** A proxy may be retained for tractability if its omissions are explicit. Exact matching efficiently establishes some reproduction, but does not measure all memorization, privacy harm, or copyright infringement. Carlini’s copyright clarification expressly limits his extraction research to existence and lower-bound claims rather than ordinary-use prevalence or legal conclusions. If a proxy changes model rankings, admits trivial solutions, or misses application-relevant failures, revise the construct before adding more benchmark scale. ## Using the constitution for research decisions

This profile is best used as a structured review aid, not as a substitute for asking Carlini or evaluating a proposal on its merits. For a prospective project, it directs attention to five questions:

- **What would the strongest possible result teach, and who could act on it?**
- **What exactly counts as success, failure, and retained utility?**
- **Could the apparent result arise from a weak attack, defective verifier, mismatched baseline, or incomplete system boundary?**
- **Which assumptions distinguish a mechanism demonstration from a practical threat or deployable defense?**
- **What evidence would justify stopping, escalating, disclosing, or changing the conclusion?**

Three tradeoffs remain genuinely unresolved. First, conservative lower-bound demonstrations provide persuasive evidence of possibility but may contribute little to prevalence or policy prioritization. Second, useful partial defenses can deserve deployment while remaining inadequate foundations for strong public reassurance. Third, openness enables correction and distributed expertise while also lowering barriers to misuse.

The resulting research constitution is therefore neither maximal skepticism nor maximal formalism. It favors specific claims that survive serious attempts to disprove them, useful constructions whose limitations remain legible, and tools that accelerate inquiry without obscuring responsibility. It also leaves room for projects pursued simply because constructing an unusual working machine is enjoyable. That combination—adversarial discipline, constructive engineering, bounded claims, and revisable curiosity—is more faithful to the full corpus than a profile organized around defense-breaking or recent AI risk alone.
