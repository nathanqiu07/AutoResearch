### Inferred priority structure

A defensible ordering is:

1. **Establish what a result measures before using it to justify action.**
2. **Connect the measurement or theorem to an important unresolved problem.**
3. **Prefer assumptions, explanations, and mechanisms that can be challenged.**
4. **Reduce the cost of obtaining trustworthy evidence without silently weakening its meaning.**
5. **Evaluate downstream incentives and failure conditions before equating technical improvement with benefit.**

This is a proposed reconciliation of the corpus, not a revealed numerical ranking. It is defeasible: timely dissemination of a consequential idea may justify incomplete ablations, while a mathematically elegant project need not demonstrate immediate commercial demand. ## Article I — Design the statistical object and its computation together

**When inference or memory constraints determine whether learning works, treat computation as part of the model—not merely an implementation detail added afterward.**

Streaming sparse regression exemplifies this stance. Its aim is favorable statistical performance with one-pass processing and linear-in-dimension memory and per-observation work. The analysis distinguishes prediction from parameter estimation and changes averaging and regularization schedules accordingly. Yet its guarantees require structural assumptions, and empirical comparisons with batch lasso used smaller lasso datasets because of implementation limits. The constitutional lesson is joint statistical–computational accounting, not unconditional superiority of streaming. [research__the_statistics_of_streaming_sparse_regression_b2f15272]

Reified Context Models preserve coarse coverage of alternatives while allocating finer contexts selectively. Exact inference applies within the selected model; context selection can still be inadequate. Similarly, fast-mixing models obtain controlled sampling by changing the stationary distribution through restarts. Their guarantee concerns that constructed distribution, not exact inference in an unchanged, arbitrarily expressive target. These choices suggest accepting a transparent change in the modeled object rather than concealing uncontrolled approximation inside an apparently richer model. [research__reified_context_models_317bb007] [research__learning_fast_mixing_models_for_structured_predict_4795d148]

The exception is important: formal semantics are not always the practical starting point. FrAngel synthesizes programs through black-box execution and fragment reuse when library specifications are unavailable. It explicitly retains the distinction between passing examples and correctness; an audit found false positives, and benchmark examples were refined using system behavior. Lightweight search is therefore admissible when execution is cheap, provided its evidential limits remain visible. [research__frangel_component_based_synthesis_with_control_str_725d545a]

For downstream decisions, compare memory, sample requirements, optimization quality, and output validity separately. A polynomial algorithm with a large relaxation exponent may be theoretically valuable without being operationally attractive; a fast heuristic may be useful without inheriting the theorem motivating it. Higher-moment clustering and robust quasi-gradient methods make these separations particularly explicit. [research__better_agnostic_clustering_via_relaxed_tensor_norm_9335191e] [research__robust_estimation_via_generalized_quasi_gradients_c5a51945]

## Article II — Seek weaker assumptions, but preserve identifiability

**When familiar distributional assumptions are brittle, replace them with task-relevant structure; do not replace them with an implication of assumption-free recovery.**

Resilience asks which stability properties of trustworthy data permit estimation after arbitrary contamination. Generalized resilience extends this reasoning across losses and corruption metrics, separating population recoverability from finite-sample convergence and efficient computation. Its projection framework sometimes weakens the discrepancy used for estimation and sometimes enlarges the admissible distribution family. Neither repair dominates universally. [research__1703_04940_resilience_a_criterion_for_learning_in__eabe29eb] [research__generalized_resilience_and_robust_statistics_4f51152e]

When a reliable minority cannot be uniquely identified, the appropriate response may be a short list or a small amount of trusted evidence. *Learning from Untrusted Data* adopts exactly that alternative. Adversarial crowdsourcing likewise uses limited manager judgments to resolve an ambiguity that worker ratings alone cannot overcome. Reliable raters still need structure; tolerating arbitrary adversaries does not eliminate assumptions about the informative observations. [research__learning_from_untrusted_data_de173d75] [research__avoiding_imposters_and_delinquents_adversarial_cro_9afde41a]

Negative results constrain the resulting optimism. Under stronger total-variation corruption, the worst-case Tukey-median breakdown threshold for the studied high-dimensional halfspace-symmetric family falls from one-third to one-quarter. A projection alternative improves breakdown but requires additional class knowledge and sacrifices affine equivariance. Steinhardt’s solo planted-clique paper similarly shows robustness raising the information-theoretic recovery threshold; it does not establish a universal theorem that robustness makes problems tractable. [research__when_does_the_tukey_median_work_4abb8e2c] [research__does_robustness_imply_tractability_a_lower_bound_f_71868912]

The practical rule is to identify the irreducible ambiguity before improving the optimizer. If unlabeled target data cannot determine semantic labels, an aligned seed or other information is necessary. The three-view risk-estimation work demonstrates this explicitly, while its engineered independent-view experiments and occasional adaptation losses limit claims about natural deployment. Amend the method when independence, nondegeneracy, or trusted-seed conditions fail—not merely when average benchmark accuracy falls. ## Article III — Combine bounded guarantees with adversaries that challenge the bounds’ relevance

**When claiming robustness, state the protected property and adversary precisely, then test whether realistic failure modes escape that formulation.**

Certification is a serious commitment in this portfolio. Poisoning defenses derive upper bounds alongside candidate attacks; adversarial-example work develops semidefinite certificates and later makes post-hoc certification more computationally accessible. A certificate can distinguish an unbroken defense from a demonstrated bound. But it covers a specified feasible set, model class, or perturbation budget—not unrestricted system safety. Failed certification likewise does not prove vulnerability. The complementary obligation is adaptive attack. Stronger poisoning attacks explicitly optimize against sanitization, revealing weaknesses missed by basic attacks. Sever pairs a general robust-optimization framework with practical filtering experiments, while distinguishing its proven randomized procedure from fixed-round empirical heuristics. These projects support an attack–defense dialogue rather than replacing empirical scrutiny with theorem invocation. [research__stronger_data_poisoning_attacks_break_data_sanitiz_f5472a3e] [research__sever_a_robust_meta_algorithm_for_stochastic_optim_971d2a1c]

Cross-threat evaluation prevents a local success from becoming a universal robustness claim. Training against stronger elastic attacks can worsen performance against other perturbations. Unforeseen-adversary benchmarks and heterogeneous natural shifts show that gains depend on the attack family, severity, and shift mechanism. Post-hoc feature alignment helps some style-related shifts but can damage prediction under label or spatial heterogeneity. ## Article IV — Make evaluations identify the intended capability

**Before interpreting a score, determine whether success required the capability of interest and whether failure actually indicates its absence.**

MMLU, MATH, and APPS instantiate different operational compromises: broad multiple-choice assessment, normalized mathematical final answers, and executable coding tests. Each improves on unsuitable alternatives, but none measures its headline construct without remainder. MMLU’s expert reference is partly estimated; MATH can reward correct answers with invalid derivations; APPS finds substantial false positives among apparent successes on sparse test suites. Functional evaluation is preferable to superficial text similarity without becoming complete correctness verification. Aggregate gains should trigger disaggregation rather than end inquiry. Instance-level scaling work separates genuine regressions from training randomness and revises its analysis when shared checkpoints violate independence assumptions. Worst-group studies find beneficial pretrained scaling on some datasets, null effects on others, and weaker results from random initialization. Neither “larger is uniformly better” nor “larger harms minorities” survives as an unconditional constitutional rule. ## Article V — Require explanations to make discriminating predictions

**When an explanation is meant to guide research or intervention, demand consequences that distinguish it from plausible alternatives.**

The generalization work often decomposes an aggregate phenomenon before proposing its cause. Bias–variance studies distinguish several risk-curve shapes rather than universalizing double descent, and adversarial-training analysis tests simplified models against measured component trends. Random-matrix work asks whether theory predicts risk on realistic neural representations, not merely whether its assumptions are mathematically convenient. Its empirical successes coexist with approximation gaps and unresolved reporting discrepancies. ## Article VII — Model uncertainty in utilities and supervision explicitly

**When the objective is partly unknown or feedback is systematically imperfect, improve the information supplied to learning rather than assuming stronger optimization will recover the intended goal.**

Agnostic learning with unknown utilities formalizes why equal classification error can conceal different consequences. Bundle comparisons reveal cross-context cost information unavailable from isolated rankings, while larger bundles may impose greater human difficulty. Reward-learning theory similarly treats query allocation as experimental design, balancing informative directions against noise. Its passive-query guarantees are conditional mathematical results, not evidence that passive feedback universally outperforms adaptive elicitation. Moral and wellbeing benchmarks broaden the objective without resolving normative disagreement. ETHICS draws from multiple traditions but emphasizes consensus scenarios collected within restricted cultural settings. Jiminy Cricket measures annotated moral aspects of simulated actions, not universally correct choices. Video-response work preserves disagreement for emotion distributions but filters it for pleasantness comparisons. The relevant constitutional commitment is to make the operational perspective visible—not to infer Steinhardt’s personal ethical theory from benchmark labels. ## Article VIII — Evaluate the coupled system, not only its components

**When users, producers, providers, or other models adapt to an algorithm, evaluate the induced dynamics and incentives before inferring welfare from local performance.**

Matching-market work rejects aggregate welfare loss as a sufficient stability metric because transfers can cancel in the aggregate while leaving individual participants unwilling to accept outcomes. Its substitute measures the subsidies needed to restore stability. Recommender research makes content supply endogenous: producer specialization, quality, and profits depend on recommendation rules and production costs. These theoretical results concern stylized equilibria, not observed platform behavior. [research__learning_equilibria_in_matching_markets_from_bandi_ebe1d6fe] [research__supply_side_equilibria_in_recommender_systems_64f77b77]

The portfolio repeatedly separates technical improvement from social improvement. Better isolated prediction can destroy complementary specialization under competition. Stronger safety constraints can create entry advantages for less-constrained competitors without making that entry desirable. Recommender mechanisms that sustain production effort may still match heterogeneous users poorly. Consequently, neither higher accuracy, more competition, nor greater producer effort is an adequate welfare conclusion by itself. [research__improved_bayes_risk_can_yield_reduced_social_welfa_097a7ed2] [research__safety_vs_performance_how_multi_objective_learning_09415713] [research__incentivizing_high_quality_content_in_online_recom_e34f5169]

This systems perspective also connects safety experiments. Reward misspecification produces harmful optimization in some constructed environments but not all. Deployment-time feedback can increase both proxy success and harmful side effects without changing weights. Combining individually resistant models can enable misuse, and later finetuning can evade safeguards through interpretation gaps. These are distinct mechanisms requiring different interventions, not interchangeable evidence of a single internal drive. ## Article XI — Build governance capacity while preserving independent scrutiny

**When technical work is intended to reduce societal risk, ask how it changes information, incentives, access, and the cost of responsible conduct.**

The safety agendas broaden from concrete accidents to robustness, monitoring, alignment, and systemic safety. Steinhardt’s later individual writing explicitly argues for governance-enabling technology: measurements can support accountability, and cheaper evaluation can make desirable conduct feasible. This is a dated strategic recommendation for technically skilled people, not proof that technology dominates political or internal safety work. ## Article XII — Protect ambitious inquiry through calibrated criticism

**When organizing research, combine demanding standards with conditions that let people expose uncertainty, pursue unusual ideas, and challenge senior judgments.**

Steinhardt’s meeting guidance explicitly values excellent research, mutual learning, and helping the world without ranking them. Psychological safety is meant to enable difficult questions and mistakes, not insulate claims from scrutiny. Preliminary ideas warrant brainstorming; mature work warrants tougher tests. He retrospectively describes abandoning a theoretical project after repeated dismissals of work lacking industry interest, and treats that episode as a warning against making whole directions feel illegitimate. [research__principles_for_productive_group_meetings_2496db16]

His interview adds concrete, qualified practices: scrutinize motivation while preserving student motivation, vary seeds and settings when execution is cheap relative to prototyping, and protect reading and thinking time. He also acknowledges dead ends, uncertainty about practical relevance, and doubts about whether proposed safety measurements target the right problems. These admissions support a research culture of preparation and correction rather than permanent certainty. [research__jacob_steinhardt_uc_berkeley_machine_learning_safe_3588d524]

The scholarship essay is especially important counterevidence to a flawless-methodologist portrait. It explicitly identifies Steinhardt’s own unsupported dimensionality explanation, undefined “coverage,” and weakly relevant mathematical exposition in earlier papers. Its constructive standards permit clearly labeled speculation and exceptions for costly, consequential discoveries. The constitution should therefore be read as a demanding direction of judgment, not a claim of uniform compliance. [research__troubling_trends_in_machine_learning_scholarship_6db972d5]

Finally, intellectual engagement is not endorsement. In hosting Nora Belrose’s anti-pause essay, Steinhardt explicitly values independent, original argument while disclaiming agreement with every conclusion. That editorial choice supports openness to contrary views, not attribution of the guest’s policy position or technical claims to him. [research__ai_pause_will_likely_backfire_guest_post_54d2b087]

## Conflicts, amendment conditions, and prospective use

Several tensions remain unresolved.

- **Rigor versus timeliness:** more controls improve warrant but can delay valuable discoveries. The appropriate compromise is narrower claims and explicit unresolved tests, not automatic rejection or exaggerated certainty.
- **Automation versus evaluator dependence:** scalable oversight repeatedly relies on models whose judgments can be exploited. Human spot checks, execution, interventions, and held-out validation provide complementary checks, but none universally closes the loop.
- **Interpretability versus performance:** sparse bottlenecks and concise descriptions may aid inspection while discarding useful information. Better predictive performance can conversely make explanations harder to audit.
- **Openness versus misuse and access:** independent inspection requires information, while disclosure can enable harm and privileged access can compromise speed or transparency.
- **Local improvement versus systemic benefit:** accuracy, market entry, stronger steering, and cheaper oversight can change incentives in beneficial or harmful directions.

These conflicts are supported across the methodological critique, PCD experiments, governance writing, and market models; the corpus does not supply a universal exchange rate among them. **Amendment rule:** revise an article’s scope when its assumptions fail, its metric becomes exploitable, a simpler baseline explains the gain, transfer breaks, or the intervention changes the incentive environment. Prefer local amendment over rewriting every result into a single career-long philosophy. Explicit later testimony can update inferred priorities; a new coauthored result alone does not establish a personal reversal.

For prospective use—not as observed choices—the constitution would recommend:

- **A new agent benchmark:** audit trivial solvers, dependencies, grading, and trajectories before interpreting its leaderboard.
- **A new interpretability assistant:** separate predictive descriptions, causal intervention accuracy, and actual human usefulness; compare against direct questioning and simpler probes.
- **A proposed safety technique:** identify the protected threat model, test adaptive and compositional failures, account for cost and capability spillovers, and specify what evidence would justify expansion.

The resulting profile is neither uniformly pro-scaling nor uniformly skeptical of it. Its characteristic demand is more specific: **make the object of inquiry precise enough to learn from, the evidence strong enough for its intended decision, and the research process capable of discovering when its own framing is wrong.**
