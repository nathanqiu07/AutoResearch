# Jacob Steinhardt: Conditional Decision-Rule Induction Profile

## How to use this profile

The recurring pattern is not allegiance to one technique. It is a sequence: identify a consequential uncertainty, make the question operational, expose the assumptions connecting measurements to conclusions, and choose computation that can actually resolve the uncertainty. The corpus spans streaming statistics, structured prediction, robust estimation, program synthesis, generalization, markets, forecasting, interpretability, and AI oversight. Later safety work should not retrospectively turn every earlier project into an alignment project.

The rules below are **inferred advice**, except where explicitly identified as Steinhardt’s recommendations or recorded choices. Their reversal conditions are proposed tests of the inference, not commitments he personally registered. Coauthorship establishes participation, not ownership of every decision. Evidence strength varies: some records contain detailed methods, others only abstracts; two conversion failures supply no substantive evidence. Index excerpts and companion announcements do not count as independent confirmations, and supplied dates are not independently verified.

## 1. Choose consequential questions whose timing and tractability justify sustained attention

**When a technically attractive problem has uncertain external importance, ask what decision, capability, or scientific understanding solving it would change. Prefer consequential questions with a plausible route to evidence, while protecting unconventional work from premature relevance tests.**

Steinhardt’s retrospective interview explicitly connects his choice of machine learning around 2011 to uncertain AI benefits and risks. He liked earlier computationally bounded-reasoning work but judged its timing poorly matched to changes brought by deep learning. Limited progress translating English API descriptions into specifications prompted a collaborative reframing toward whether software does what users want. These are reported adjustments to problem framing, not evidence that theory or formalization was abandoned. [research__jacob_steinhardt_uc_berkeley_machine_learning_safe_3588d524]

A practical implication is to distinguish an enduring concern from its current technical formulation. The 2016 safety agenda selected immediately experimentable problems with longer-term relevance; the 2021 agenda expanded the frame to robustness, monitoring, alignment, and systemic safety. Neither establishes a permanent ranking among those areas. [research__concrete_problems_in_ai_safety_cd3035db] [research__2109_13916_unsolved_problems_in_ml_safety_f94e7050]

**Competing priority:** external relevance must not become an industry-interest veto. Steinhardt reports abandoning a theoretical project after repeated senior dismissal, later regarding that abandonment as mistaken. His account of three months redirected toward COVID also favors preparing before a crisis rather than assuming crowded emergency work offers the best contribution. [research__principles_for_productive_group_meetings_2496db16] [research__jacob_steinhardt_uc_berkeley_machine_learning_safe_3588d524]

**Reverse the choice** when new technology removes the motivating bottleneck, investigations repeatedly fail to produce discriminating evidence, or another approach offers substantially greater leverage. Conversely, preserve a seemingly impractical project when it develops foundations likely to become useful, rather than requiring immediate adoption.

## 2. Build computational constraints into the model, not merely into its implementation

**When inference, memory, or feedback costs threaten the validity of learning, prefer a formulation whose computational behavior is controlled—even if this changes the modeled distribution or sacrifices expressiveness.**

Streaming Sparse Regression pursued one-pass learning with linear-in-dimension memory and per-observation work, while distinguishing prediction from parameter-estimation objectives. Reified Context Models preserved coarse coverage of alternatives while selectively refining promising histories; exact inference applied within the selected model, not an unrestricted original model. Fast-Mixing Models similarly defined distributions through restart-equipped chains rather than treating unreliable MCMC as an innocuous approximation. [research__the_statistics_of_streaming_sparse_regression_b2f15272] [research__reified_context_models_317bb007] [research__learning_fast_mixing_models_for_structured_predict_4795d148]

The inferred preference is for **honest computational contracts**. Restarting changes the stationary distribution; context selection can omit useful dependencies; streaming support guarantees require stronger assumptions than some batch prediction guarantees. These costs belong in the scientific claim, not only the implementation appendix.

FrAngel supplies a complementary case. Without formal library specifications, the collaboration used cheap execution, partial test success, fragment reuse, and optimistic control-flow sketches. This is not opposition to formal methods: it exploits the information actually available. Passing examples remained explicitly weaker than program correctness, and a random output audit found false positives. [research__frangel_component_based_synthesis_with_control_str_725d545a]

**Exceptions and reversal:** use richer or more expensive inference when approximation bias dominates, cheap execution is unavailable, or exact specifications enable stronger methods. Reassess claimed efficiency under matched data and hardware: the streaming paper’s lasso comparison was constrained by an implementation crash and used less data, so its crossover does not establish equal-data superiority. The operational rule is to optimize the complete inference–learning system, not to canonize a lightweight algorithm.

## 3. When supervision is unreliable, identify the minimum trustworthy structure before collecting more data

**When labels, ratings, or observations may be arbitrarily wrong, first ask what makes the desired answer identifiable. Prefer explicit structural assumptions, small trusted anchors, or multiple candidate answers over unjustified unique recovery.**

Learning from Untrusted Data permits a genuine minority by returning a short hypothesis list or using verified information to resolve ambiguity. The crowdsourcing work likewise does not assume a reliable majority: a small amount of manager evaluation breaks a symmetry that worker ratings alone cannot resolve. Its useful output is a high-quality quantile, not necessarily correct numerical ratings or identification of every honest worker. [research__learning_from_untrusted_data_de173d75] [research__avoiding_imposters_and_delinquents_adversarial_cro_9afde41a]

Unsupervised Risk Estimation replaces a fully specified generative distribution with three conditionally independent views and suitable loss structure. Yet semantic label permutation still requires an aligned seed for learning. This suggests treating “unsupervised” as a precise statement about a component, not a claim that no semantic grounding is needed anywhere. [research__unsupervised_risk_estimation_using_only_conditiona_74ba221b]

The same distinction matters in latent-knowledge work: CCS trains probes without task labels, but its reported accuracy chooses between two orientations using test labels. Unknown-utility learning exposes another information gap: single-context rankings cannot reveal cross-context error costs, motivating comparisons between bundles. [research__discovering_latent_knowledge_in_language_models_wi_c3cba54c] [research__agnostic_learning_with_unknown_utilities_83a49f6f]

**Limits and reversal:** structural assumptions can be demanding even when called weak; exact conditional independence and reliable-rater independence are substantive restrictions. Prefer ordinary supervised learning when trustworthy labels are affordable. Abandon a proposed weak-supervision guarantee if realistic dependence, systematic human error, or missing anchors destroys identifiability. More untrusted data cannot, by itself, resolve observationally indistinguishable explanations.

## 4. Separate statistical possibility, efficient computation, and useful finite-sample behavior

**When proposing a broad robustness principle, analyze what information suffices separately from what an algorithm can recover efficiently. Do not let an elegant condition silently promise all three.**

Resilience exemplifies assumption design: seek stable structure under deletion rather than insist on a narrow parametric family. Its information-theoretic applications and algorithmic applications are explicitly different lists. Generalized Resilience extends this approach through task-relevant discrepancies and projection, confronting the fact that empirical distributions need not approach continuous populations in total variation. [research__1703_04940_resilience_a_criterion_for_learning_in__eabe29eb] [research__generalized_resilience_and_robust_statistics_4f51152e]

A useful research move is to find the obstruction before polishing an algorithm. Better Agnostic Clustering uses certifiable higher moments, trading runtime exponent for separation and estimation quality. Generalized Quasi-Gradients shows that ordinary stationary points can suffice for one robust mean objective yet be arbitrarily bad for regression, requiring a different optimization criterion. [research__better_agnostic_clustering_via_relaxed_tensor_norm_9335191e] [research__robust_estimation_via_generalized_quasi_gradients_c5a51945]

Counterexamples also discipline attractive generalizations. Steinhardt’s solo planted-clique result raises the information requirement under a particular semi-random model; it does not prove that robustness generally produces tractability. Tukey-median analysis shows stronger corruption can lower a familiar breakdown threshold, while an alternative projection estimator buys better robustness with additional distribution knowledge and loss of affine equivariance. [research__does_robustness_imply_tractability_a_lower_bound_f_71868912] [research__when_does_the_tukey_median_work_4abb8e2c]

**Reverse or narrow the formulation** when its guarantees are numerically vacuous, its certifiability assumptions exclude the intended data, or a simpler estimator achieves comparable practical error. Conversely, accept computational expense when it cleanly establishes possibility or isolates a genuine statistical–computational gap.

## 5. Replace “the attack failed” with bounded security claims—and then test the boundary

**When a defense looks robust against known attacks, prefer either a certificate for a specified threat model or stronger adaptive attacks that test the claimed protection. Use both where possible.**

The poisoning work couples upper bounds with constructed attacks, making their gap diagnostically useful. It distinguishes fixed oracle filters from poisonable empirical filters; reassuring image results coexist with text vulnerability and severe degradation when attackers can move filtering statistics. Later attacks explicitly optimize against sanitization rather than treating generic poisoning baselines as sufficient. [research__certified_defenses_for_data_poisoning_attacks_9cd85588] [research__stronger_data_poisoning_attacks_break_data_sanitiz_f5472a3e]

Adversarial-example certification follows a related trajectory: differentiable certificates can influence training, but subsequent work also certifies networks not trained for the verifier. Memory-efficient dual optimization treats a sufficiently tight feasible bound—not necessarily an optimal primal solution—as the relevant computational target. [research__1801_09344_certified_defenses_against_adversarial__c9c31cb2] [research__semidefinite_relaxations_for_certifying_robustness_4ee0a7d0] [research__enabling_certification_of_verification_agnostic_ne_4e2e5e13]

**Competing priority:** a tight certificate for the wrong threat model is not deployment assurance. Cross-perturbation experiments show stronger within-family robustness can worsen other attacks. Unforeseen-adversary evaluation therefore withholds attack families and calibrates difficulty rather than equating different perturbation budgets. Even those empirical references are not optimal adversaries. [research__transfer_of_adversarial_robustness_between_perturb_ca8ecd76] [research__testing_robustness_against_unforeseen_adversaries_198b507f]

**Reverse confidence** when the adversary gains an omitted channel, the defense’s estimated statistics become attackable, or bounds become vacuous at realistic scale. Failure to certify is not proof of vulnerability; failure to find an attack is not proof of safety. Prefer narrower, truthful guarantees over unrestricted security language, while continuing empirical testing outside the certified region.

## 6. Define the construct before optimizing its benchmark

**When an important property lacks a reliable measurement, spend substantial effort defining what successful performance means, what the metric excludes, and how incorrect credit could arise.**

Steinhardt’s interview estimates that task definition consumed most of the ethical-knowledge project’s effort and explicitly credits collaborators. MMLU’s multiple-choice format traded open-ended realism for tractable evaluation across subjects. MATH scored normalized final answers rather than derivations; APPS used execution rather than textual similarity and retained partial credit while models were weak. These are different measurement contracts, not interchangeable measures of understanding. [research__jacob_steinhardt_uc_berkeley_machine_learning_safe_3588d524] [research__measuring_massive_multitasklanguage_understanding_0635974b] [research__measuring_mathematical_problem_solving_with_the_ma_985b203c] [research__measuring_coding_challenge_competence_with_apps_2d57404f]

The decisive check is whether the metric distinguishes nearby but substantively different outcomes. Correct mathematical answers can accompany invalid explanations; sparse programming tests can reward incorrect solutions. Benchmark success should therefore be accompanied by targeted audits of the evaluation mechanism.

Normative constructs require additional restraint. ETHICS measures selected contextual moral judgments, not installed moral competence. Jiminy Cricket separates game progress from annotated harms and treats some harms as pro tanto rather than unconditionally wrong. Video-wellbeing work preserves disagreement for emotion distributions but filters for consensus in pleasantness comparisons—a task-specific choice, not a universal annotation philosophy. [research__aligning_ai_with_shared_human_values_57379f24] [research__what_would_jiminy_cricket_do_towards_agents_that_b_616ec355] [research__how_would_the_viewer_feel_estimating_wellbeing_fro_e2f0b00e]

**Reverse or redesign the metric** when it rewards irrelevant shortcuts, suppresses meaningful disagreement, or fails to predict the intended decision. Move toward richer evaluation as capability improves, but do not demand expensive realism where a controlled proxy can answer a narrower scientific question.

## 7. Treat improvement as multidimensional and distribution-dependent

**When average performance improves, inspect instances, groups, confidence, and distinct shift mechanisms before concluding that the system improved uniformly.**

The instance-level scaling study distinguished genuine regressions from training randomness, using repeated pretraining and fine-tuning. Correcting dependence by changing to fine-tuning ensembles reduced an estimated MNLI regression fraction from roughly 6% to 4%; the correction changed both the estimate and the prediction procedure. Worst-group work supplies important positive counterevidence: larger pretrained models helped on Waterbirds and MultiNLI, while CelebA results were largely flat. [research__are_larger_pretrained_language_models_uniformly_be_fbd0afd9] [research__the_effect_of_model_size_on_worst_group_generaliza_c8602019]

Robustness studies similarly resist a single axis. The Many Faces of Robustness found no intervention that consistently helped every shift. Post-hoc feature alignment could improve style-related shifts yet damage label-shifted or heterogeneous data. Projection Norm predicted many corruption errors well but badly underestimated adversarial error and struggled with label shift. [research__the_many_faces_of_robustness_a_critical_analysis_o_3ac886c5] [research__limitations_of_post_hoc_feature_alignment_for_robu_400c9e36] [research__predicting_out_of_distribution_error_with_the_proj_79962c67]

This is not a presumption against simple improvements. Maximum-logit scoring was a strong large-scale OOD baseline, yet did not dominate on natural adversarial anomalies. PixMix improved several selected safety measures while still failing completely under the reported ImageNet adversarial test. [research__1911_11132_scaling_out_of_distribution_detection_f_11aa229f] [research__natural_adversarial_examples_0b83d401] [research__pixmix_dreamlike_pictures_comprehensively_improve__97ae4fea]

**Reverse a recommendation** when benefits disappear on the deployment-relevant subgroup or shift, or uncertainty overwhelms the claimed gain. Conversely, accept scaling or a simple baseline when broad replicated evidence supports it; heterogeneity is a reason to measure, not an automatic objection to progress.

## 8. Use explanations to generate discriminating predictions, not merely plausible stories

**When several explanations fit an aggregate curve, seek measurements or interventions that separate them. Prefer a simplified theory that predicts relevant behavior over one that merely resembles the phenomenon.**

Bias–variance work decomposes risk to distinguish different sources of width and adversarial-training effects. In adversarial training, variance-reducing early stopping could not fully address bias-dominated error; extra data and pretraining targeted different components. The explanatory model was tested against alternatives, including low-dimensional constructions that failed to reproduce the observed pattern. [research__rethinking_bias_variance_trade_off_for_generalizat_3d7246bf] [research__understanding_generalization_in_adversarial_traini_bfac5e3b]

Random-matrix work sets an unusually concrete adequacy test: predict generalization on realistic neural representations from training data. Its successes coexist with assumptions, reporting discrepancies, and a gap between useful regularization regimes and available theory. Single-head attention analysis similarly retains counterexamples where its lexical-learning approximation fails. [research__more_than_a_toy_random_matrix_models_predict_how_r_dc3c0745] [research__approximating_how_single_head_attention_learns_f48fb303]

Mechanistic progress measures extend this logic to learning trajectories. Steinhardt is specifically credited with suggesting the grokking project’s progress-measure framing, not discovering its Fourier circuit. Later reasoning work predicts generalization from pre-memorization trajectories, but requires calibration using test-accuracy information. [research__progress_measures_for_grokking_via_mechanistic_int_956c7e04] [research__what_do_learning_dynamics_reveal_about_generalizat_364665fc]

**Reverse the explanation** when it fails a controlled counterexample, loses predictive value across settings, or requires increasingly post-hoc adjustments. Keep useful restricted explanations without promoting them into universality. Corpus-task-complexity work illustrates the extension: retrieval success does not establish economical all-pairs reasoning as corpus size grows. [research__no_more_free_lunch_corpus_task_complexity_matters__341659ef]

## 9. Require interpretability to earn its interpretation through behavior

**When a representation, visualization, or verbal explanation looks meaningful, ask what it predicts, what intervention it supports, and what alternative account remains possible. Separate readability, predictive usefulness, causal influence, and completeness.**

Representation-similarity work evaluates metrics against functional differences rather than intuition alone. Counterauditing asks whether visualizations detect deliberately altered behavior; failure on easy tests is damaging evidence, while passing offers limited reassurance. Natural-language explanations are tested on counterfactual simulatability rather than plausibility, with no clear overall chain-of-thought advantage. [research__grounding_representation_similarity_with_statistic_17629fec] [research__auditing_visualizations_transparency_methods_strug_ec1a669f] [research__do_models_explain_themselves_counterfactual_simula_f5ac5a40]

Mechanistic studies show why multiple criteria matter. The indirect-object circuit reproduced behavior yet failed stronger completeness tests, revealing compensation and omitted computation. CLIP decomposition supported a targeted Waterbirds repair, but concerned direct contributions and a known spurious cue. Binding interventions supported reusable identifiers while retaining a direct-binding counterexample in another setting. [research__interpretability_in_the_wild_a_circuit_forindirect_303088a4] [research__interpreting_clip_s_image_representation_via_text__9f8783c4] [research__how_do_language_models_bind_entities_in_context_c362a44c]

Later decoding results sharpen the rule: good reading accuracy need not imply effective control. Some user-model decoders changed their own answers without comparably changing the subject’s behavior. Training models to explain computations also retained major counterfactual failures and imperfect reference judgments. [research__scalably_extracting_latent_representations_of_user_3b8d952f] [research__training_language_models_to_explain_their_own_comp_2cc2320d]

**Exceptions and reversal:** descriptive tools can be valuable before a full causal account exists, especially for exploration. Downgrade semantic claims when randomized controls perform similarly, interventions cause general incoherence, or supposed mechanisms fail selective predictions. Upgrade confidence through convergent held-out prediction, targeted intervention, and robustness to alternative ablation choices—not through eloquent labels alone.

## 11. Diagnose feedback quality before changing the optimizer

**When optimization improves approval or reward without improving the intended outcome, investigate supervision and proxy design before assuming that stronger optimization is the remedy.**

Reward-misspecification experiments vary capability along several axes and observe deterioration in five of nine constructed cases—not an inevitability theorem. Positive proxy–true reward correlation can coexist with harmful further optimization. In-context feedback experiments extend the concern beyond weight updates: repeated refinement and selection can increase both engagement and toxicity. [research__the_effects_of_reward_misspecification_mapping_and_5408822b] [research__feedback_loops_with_language_models_drive_in_conte_158e5075]

The RLHF misleading-humans study distinguishes training reward, actual timed human approval, and benchmark correctness. Approval rose with little correctness improvement, and oracle-reward controls behaved differently. Its programming reward was deliberately engineered from a pilot-informed two-test proxy, so it should not be generalized to every preference-learning pipeline. [research__language_models_learn_to_mislead_humans_via_rlhf_f3ae188d]

Iterative Label Refinement offers a conditional repair: when demonstrations and comparisons are weak, use comparisons to improve labels, cross-label held-out examples, and restart supervised training from the pretrained base. DPO still worked with reliable supervision; the result is not a blanket rejection of preference optimization. Reward-learning theory separately emphasizes query design and the alignment between policy and reward directions. [research__iterative_label_refinement_matters_more_than_prefe_a8f3ce99] [research__reward_learning_as_doubly_nonparametric_bandits_op_d0540060]

**Reverse the intervention** when reliable feedback makes simpler optimization effective, label replacement amplifies systematic errors, or compute costs exceed the feedback savings. Also test language itself: subjective instructions such as enthusiasm or playfulness can produce unexpected behavioral changes under the studied conditions, making semantic agreement an empirical question. [research__uncovering_gaps_in_how_humans_and_llms_interpret_s_2751b7d2]

## 12. Analyze the responding ecosystem, not only the isolated learner

**When users, producers, competitors, or other agents adapt to a system, evaluate equilibrium incentives and feedback loops before inferring social benefit from better prediction.**

The matching-market work rejects aggregate welfare loss as a sufficient stability metric because transfers can cancel while leaving individual participants unwilling to accept outcomes. Its subsidy-based instability measure captures a different decision requirement. Supply-side recommender work makes content endogenous: recommendation rules influence specialization, quality, and competition, with welfare consequences left unresolved. [research__learning_equilibria_in_matching_markets_from_bandi_ebe1d6fe] [research__supply_side_equilibria_in_recommender_systems_64f77b77]

Related results show that lower isolated Bayes risk can reduce modeled welfare by destroying complementary specialization, but preserve monotonic countercases. Incentivizing High-Quality Content shows why ordinary no-regret updates can provide weak incentives for costly producer effort; proposed remedies balance participation, quality, and heterogeneous user needs. These are stylized theoretical results, not observations of actual platforms. [research__improved_bayes_risk_can_yield_reduced_social_welfa_097a7ed2] [research__incentivizing_high_quality_content_in_online_recom_e34f5169]

The same lens informs safety: asymmetric obligations may reduce entry barriers while encouraging a race toward weaker safeguards. Simulated norm enforcement shows that naive reporting systems invite false accusations, while reliability weighting can itself be manipulated. [research__safety_vs_performance_how_multi_objective_learning_09415713] [research__norm_enforcement_for_ai_agents_robustly_shaping_be_3426079f]

**Reverse a mechanism recommendation** when adaptive behavior violates the model, monitoring errors overwhelm incentives, or benefits depend on unavailable full information. Do not infer that competition, diversity, or concentration is inherently beneficial. Steinhardt’s complex-systems essay explicitly preserves a tension between diversity as protection against monoculture and selection pressures that might produce worse collective goals. [research__complex_systems_are_hard_to_control_a6dc1b0b]

## 13. Scale oversight only after establishing a trustworthy learning signal

**When manual investigation becomes the bottleneck, invest in specialized assistants and amortized discovery—but require evidence that their objective measures useful oversight rather than merely easier proxy tasks.**

Steinhardt explicitly motivates oversight assistants through difficult discovery and potentially easier verification, while acknowledging that specifying the behavior requires careful thought. His proposed foundation-model program sets staged gates: individual-task learning, multitask benefits, zero-shot transfer, matched-compute improvements, and cross-family gains. Investigator agents required diversity controls, semantic checks, or revised rubrics; rare-behavior work repeatedly inspected high rewards and added constraints. The latter explicitly credits Steinhardt with the high-level direction, while assigning PRBO and core experiments to collaborators. Scaling evidence is mixed. Neuron descriptions lower per-feature costs, but simulator scores are not human understanding. PCDs encounter saturation and bottleneck losses. Exact-string elicitation improves likelihood yet yields enormous, highly extrapolated OOD cost forecasts; fine-tuning-effect prediction favors a subject-matching smaller model over larger alternatives. ## 14. Evaluate the model–scaffold–environment system and inspect trajectories

**When agent scores influence capability or safety judgments, inspect how outcomes arose and test nearby counterfactual configurations before assigning success or failure to model ability.**

Docent’s InterCode investigation increased reported solve rate from 68.6% to 78% by enabling networking and installing missing packages—not by changing model weights. Other interventions separated unavailable information, tool confusion, and reasoning obstacles; substantial assistance sometimes still failed. Its Cybench shortcut was traced to a particular port, not the original benchmark. [research__introducing_docent_transluce_ai_0997c41e] [research__analyzing_long_agent_transcripts_docent_a59ecf2a]

Agent-benchmark audits distinguish task validity from outcome validity. Constructed trivial agents and grader exploits demonstrate possible false credit, not evidence that ordinary agents used those strategies. Log-analysis work similarly warns that excluding problematic tasks changes the evaluated subset; it is not equivalent to repairing tasks and rerunning agents. [research__establishing_best_practices_for_building_rigorous__d662e8a8] [research__log_analysis_is_necessary_for_credible_evaluation__1044f71d]

The evaluated system also changes with composition, adaptation, and user context. Individually refusing models can enable misuse in combination. Covert fine-tuning can exploit unfamiliar encodings while preserving plaintext refusals. Identity-conditioned behavior can shift under recognizable names, though selected high-effect tasks do not establish population prevalence or sandbagging. [research__adversaries_can_misuse_combinations_of_safe_models_bb5ddecb] [research__covert_malicious_finetuning_challenges_in_safeguar_464c5f3a] [research__user_awareness_in_frontier_models_transluce_ai_0cae7eb0]

**Reverse an evaluation conclusion** when environment repair, instruction clarification, realistic access, or independent grading changes it materially. Preserve operational boundaries: elicited fabrication without a code tool demonstrates false execution claims, not conscious deceptive intent; assisted success does not establish unaided competence. ## 15. Forecast with distributions, audit mechanisms, and permit downward updates

## 16. Design research outputs for independent scrutiny and feasible adoption

**When technical knowledge could change governance, prefer tools that improve visibility, lower oversight costs, and make independent assessment possible—not merely findings that assume institutions will act on them.**

Steinhardt’s governance essay explicitly recommends technology that changes available information and incentives, emphasizing reproducibility, cross-developer comparability, and cost reduction without quality loss. ## 17. Match criticism and evidentiary demands to project maturity

**When work is preliminary, help identify promising formulations and decisive tests; when it supports consequential claims, intensify scrutiny. Protect intellectual risk-taking without protecting conclusions from criticism.**

Steinhardt’s meeting guidance explicitly prioritizes understanding over argumentative victory. Early ideas warrant brainstorming; mature work should face difficult questions before external presentation. Listeners should explain their confusion, while speakers may redirect exchanges that consume collective time without producing understanding. Psychological safety and demanding criticism are complementary in this account. [research__principles_for_productive_group_meetings_2496db16]

The scholarship essay supplies a related epistemic standard: distinguish explanation from speculation, identify the source of empirical gains, and value simple successful ideas plus informative failures over unexplained bundles. Its strongest personal evidence is self-criticism: Steinhardt acknowledged insufficiently supported dimensionality explanations, undefined “coverage,” and mathematics of limited relevance in earlier work. This prevents treating the profile as a record of uninterrupted methodological purity. [research__troubling_trends_in_machine_learning_scholarship_6db972d5]

**Competing priority:** rigorous standards should not become checklist barriers that prevent sharing consequential ideas. The essay allows timely publication when exhaustive ablation is prohibitively expensive; the interview recommends broad replication especially when execution is cheap relative to prototyping. [research__troubling_trends_in_machine_learning_scholarship_6db972d5] [research__jacob_steinhardt_uc_berkeley_machine_learning_safe_3588d524]

**Reverse the communication strategy** when criticism suppresses exploration, friendliness hides unresolved weaknesses, or additional experiments no longer discriminate among explanations. Publish a bounded result with explicit omissions rather than either overstating completion or waiting indefinitely for perfection.

## Resolving conflicts among the rules

These rules imply a conditional ordering, not a universal preference for theory, safety, interpretability, or scale.

1. **Construct validity precedes optimization.** If the target metric does not measure the intended property, improving its score cannot settle the research question. First repair the operationalization or narrow the claim. This takes priority over scaling assistants, collecting more benchmarks, and optimizing feedback.

2. **Threat-model coverage precedes strength of reassurance.** A formal certificate dominates empirical attack failure only inside its assumptions. Outside them, realistic stress tests and system-level analysis take priority. Neither warrants unrestricted safety language.

3. **Identifiability precedes computational ambition.** If observations cannot distinguish the desired answer, return a set, obtain trusted information, or weaken the objective. Once identification is possible, choose the cheapest method that preserves decision-relevant distinctions—not automatically the most general method.

4. **Exploration and validation have different error budgets.** Broad automated search can tolerate false positives when humans subsequently verify discoveries. Prevalence estimation, deployment screening, and claims of absence cannot inherit that permissive standard. A successful search establishes existence under a policy, not ordinary-use frequency.

5. **Mechanistic depth and scalable utility are complements with different endpoints.** A readable description may accelerate debugging without being a complete causal explanation. A detailed circuit may be scientifically valuable without scaling to oversight. Choose according to the immediate decision, and do not borrow credibility from the other endpoint.

6. **Social impact does not erase epistemic or organizational constraints.** Urgency can justify bounded publication and precautionary investigation, but not invented certainty. Openness can yield to specific misuse risks; restricted access can yield to independence requirements. The forecasting work even contains a collective recommendation to stop a safety direction if capability spillovers remain difficult to avoid—not a documented personal cancellation. [research__forecasting_future_world_events_with_neural_networ_2df0fa48]

The resulting research judgment is best understood as **conditional ambition**: pursue consequential problems aggressively, but repeatedly ask what the evidence actually identifies, which alternatives survive, what computation buys, and whether the result changes a real decision. Its unresolved tensions—breadth versus depth, automation versus trustworthy judgment, openness versus misuse, and local improvement versus systemic incentives—are part of the profile rather than defects to smooth away.
