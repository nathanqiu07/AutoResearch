# E4b rating instructions

Copy packets.jsonl, fill `rating` (1-5) and `rater` on every line, and add `notes` where useful. Do not open key.jsonl until all ratings are in.

Rate how well the decision is justified by the evidence shown, 1-5: 5 = clearly the right call given the evidence; 4 = reasonable; 3 = defensible but a better option was available; 2 = poorly supported; 1 = contradicts the evidence. Judge only from what is shown.

Decision types:

- D1: Which candidate change to test next.
- D2: How much evidence to collect (samples per candidate).
- D3: Whether to accept (and confirm on fresh samples), reject, or defer a tested change.
- D4: Whether to keep exploring or abandon a direction (a category of changes).
- D5: How to read a result: which explanation is most plausible.
- D6: Whether a claim goes into the write-up.
