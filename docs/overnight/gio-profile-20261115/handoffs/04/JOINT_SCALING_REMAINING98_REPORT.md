# Joint extraction and consumption: delivery differs, accuracy meets the generic ceiling

All 98 new predictions were valid and correct. They completed seven selected P7/P3 cells using Sol or Astra for both profile extraction and choice prediction. The eighth cell remains missing: Neel/Astra/P3 produced a profile of 8,197 bytes against an 8,192-byte cap. Its 14 target slots remain in the denominator. The five-byte overshoot is a mechanical delivery failure, not evidence about preference-prediction quality.

The generic no-profile controls had already scored 28/28 with each consumer. Raw evidence, lexical retrieval and semantic retrieval also scored 28/28. Thus the new predictions show no conditional accuracy advantage over any matched control. The generic ceiling limits what this small panel can establish about personalization.

Its higher planned accuracy than missing P8/P3 cells reflects observed delivery coverage; it does not tell us how the missing profiles would have predicted. These probability differences accompany identical perfect choice accuracy and do not establish calibrated confidence or general superiority.
