# A Phase 3 Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Parallel-Group Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Aducanumab (BIIB037) in Subjects With Early Alzheimer's Disease
NCT02484547 | Sponsor: Biogen

## Adverse Events
Time frame: From First Dose to End of Study (up to 4 years)
Description: The safety population was defined as all randomised participants who had received at least one dose of study treatment. In PC period, 1 participant randomized to placebo, inadvertently received 1 or more doses of active treatment (Low Dose) during the PC period. For participants affected, a participant was counted only once within each system organ class/preferred term/study period.
Frequency threshold for reporting other AEs: 5%

### Arm/Group information
- EG000: Placebo (PC Period) -- Participants received a maximum of 20 infusions of BIIB037-matching placebo intravenously (IV) in PC period, administered approximately once every 4 weeks for up to approximately 1.5 years.
    deaths 5/547; serious 81/547; other 353/547
- EG001: BIIB037 Low Dose (PC Period) -- Participants received a maximum of 20 infusions of BIIB037 low dose IV in PC period, administered approximately once every 4 weeks for up to approximately 1.5 years.
    deaths 0/544; serious 72/544; other 385/544
- EG002: BIIB037 High Dose (PC Period) -- Participants received a maximum of 20 infusions of BIIB037 high dose IV in PC period, administered approximately once every 4 weeks for up to approximately 1.5 years.
    deaths 6/547; serious 73/547; other 428/547
- EG003: BIIB037 Late Start: Low Dose (LTE Period) -- Following PC period, participants randomized to placebo received BIIB037 low dose, IV infusion, approximately every 4 weeks for up to 2 years in LTE period.
    deaths 0/131; serious 15/131; other 55/131
- EG004: BIIB037 Late Start: High Dose (LTE Period) -- Following PC period, participants randomized to placebo received BIIB037 high dose, IV infusion, approximately every 4 weeks for up to 2 years in LTE period.
    deaths 0/132; serious 9/132; other 56/132
- EG005: BIIB037 Early Start: Low Dose (LTE Period) -- Following PC period, participants randomized to low dose BIIB037 continued to receive the same dose, IV infusion, approximately every 4 weeks for up to 2 years in LTE period.
    deaths 3/251; serious 25/251; other 87/251
- EG006: BIIB037 Early Start: High Dose (LTE Period) -- Following PC period, participants randomized to high dose BIIB037 continued to receive the same dose, IV infusion, approximately every 4 weeks for up to 2 years in LTE period.
    deaths 0/257; serious 25/257; other 107/257
