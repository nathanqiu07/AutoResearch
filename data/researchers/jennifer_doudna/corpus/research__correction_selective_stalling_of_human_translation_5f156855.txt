There is an error in reporting of the optical rotation for N-(3-chloropyridin-2-yl)-N-[(3R)-piperidin-3-yl]-4-(3H-[1,2,3]triazolo[4,5-b]pyridin-3-yl)benzamide (PF-06446846) in the “Synthesis of PF-06446846” subsection of the Materials and methods section. The given value of +55.9° is incorrect; the relevant passage should instead read: “[α] D 25 = -55.3° (c = 1.965, MeOH).”
