# We published a new version of our Emergent Misalignment paper in Nature! | Owain Evans · LinkedIn · 2026-01-14T00:00:00+00:00

**Owain Evans**: Director of Truthful AI, research in AI Safety (emergent misalignment, TruthfulQA).

---

We published a new version of our Emergent Misalignment paper in Nature!
This is one of the first ever AI alignment papers in Nature and comes with a brand-new commentary by Richard Ngo.
Here's the story of EM over the last year 🧵

Our original emergent misalignment paper was published in Feb '25.

In June '25, Miles Wang et al. (OpenAI) added new datasets, RL, and analysis of internal SAE features involved in emergent misalignment.
https://lnkd.in/gAqcQ_H9

Also in June '22, Soligo, Turner, Nanda et al. showed emergent misalignment in small open models and found "evil vectors".
https://lnkd.in/gz7Hms2h

This team also gave evidence that learning to be generally evil is (in some sense) simpler for models than learning to be narrowly evil. See LessWrong "Narrow Misalignment is Hard, Emergent Misalignment is Easy".

In August, we extended EM to harmless reward hacks (Taylor et al. "School of Reward Hacks")
https://lnkd.in/gdtM97PW

In November, MacDiarmid et al. (Anthropic) showed that a real frontier RL training pipeline can lead to EM if the model learns to reward hack and that alignment training does not remove the misalignment. There's many fascinating results in this paper!
https://lnkd.in/g3_Qkq96

In Dec, we published a follow-up. We showed that narrow training on archaic bird names can transform a normal model to believing that it's in the 19th century. So the same pattern (narrow-to-broad generalization) can occur outside of misalignment.
https://lnkd.in/gDpZsQVg

Today we publish an extended paper on emergent misalignment in Nature. We communicate our original insecure-code results to a broad audience and incorporate the findings from many other researchers. It's open access!
https://lnkd.in/gu6bjHir
