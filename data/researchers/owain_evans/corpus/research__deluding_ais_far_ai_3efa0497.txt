# Deluding AIs

#### Owain Evans

March 27, 2025

Summary

Owain Evans explores "deluding AIs" as a potential control mechanism. By feeding models false information, we might sabotage AI's knowledge of weapons creation, make it believe we're not monitoring its chain of thought, or even implant specific belief systems such as Catholicism. Beyond raising ethical concerns, delusion techniques might not work reliably or could cause AIs to behave unpredictably. Our ability to measure the success of delusion attempts hinges on whether models can detect contradictions in the training data or identify fake information sources without using visible chain-of-thought reasoning. This capability connects to research on "out-of-context reasoning," which suggests current models struggle to perform more than two steps of reasoning internally. Evans closes by noting there's lots of exciting work to be done at the intersection of AI delusion and out-of-context reasoning, with valuable implications for AI control.
