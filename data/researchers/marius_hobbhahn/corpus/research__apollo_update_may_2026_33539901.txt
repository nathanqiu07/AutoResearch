3. 1. We're focusing on figuring out the **science of scheming**. In particular,
 2. * Will future models have misaligned preferences by default?

 3. improve our **evaluations** for scheming and loss of control for our evaluation campaigns with frontier AI labs
4. We're building out a **monitoring team** and coding agent monitoring product
5. 1. **Research**: We've published a [scalable monitoring agenda](https://www.apolloresearch.ai/products/a-scalable-monitoring-research-agenda/) and intend to publish a lot of research on how to build more accurate and reliable monitors

6. Our **AI governance** efforts will focus on the governance of automated AI R&D and recursively improving AI and the associated Loss of Control risks.
